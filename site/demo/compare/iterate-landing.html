Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@tally.example. A real person answers, usually within a few hours.

Will my clients know the reminders are automated?

Only if you want them to. Reminders go out from your own email address, signed with your name, and replies land in your inbox. You can edit every template, or start from ours, which were written with freelancers who hate sounding pushy.

How does bank matching work, and is it safe?

You connect your account through a licensed open-banking provider under PSD2. Tally gets read-only access to incoming transactions. It can’t move money, and you can disconnect at any time. Access renews every 180 days, as the regulation requires.

Do you support e-invoicing in my country?

Yes for Germany (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD), France (Factur-X, ready for the 2026 reform), Italy (FatturaPA via SDI), Belgium and the Netherlands (Peppol). Everywhere else you get a compliant PDF with the mandatory mentions for your country.

I already use another tool. Can I bring my clients and invoices?

Import clients and past invoices from a CSV, or directly from most popular invoicing tools. Your numbering continues where you left off, so your accountant never sees a gap.

What happens if a client pays only part of an invoice?

Tally records the partial payment, updates the outstanding amount, and adjusts the next reminder so it asks for the balance, not the full sum.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU, on servers in Frankfurt, encrypted at rest and in transit. We never sell data or use it for advertising. You can export everything, or delete your account, in one click.
