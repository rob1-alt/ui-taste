§ 02 — Questions

Asked & answered.

Still unsure? Write to us at hello@tally.app. A human replies, usually the same day.

01Is Solo really free, or is it a trial?

It’s really free, with no time limit and no card. You can send 5 invoices every calendar month. The count resets on the 1st, and drafts and quotes don’t count towards it.

02What happens if I hit 5 invoices on Solo?

Nothing breaks. Your sent invoices, clients and payments stay exactly where they are. You can keep drafting, and the 6th invoice waits until next month or until you upgrade to Studio.

03How do automatic reminders and bank matching work?

Reminders go out politely, on a schedule you set, when an invoice is due or overdue, and they stop as soon as it’s paid. Bank matching connects your business account read-only and marks invoices paid when the transfer lands, so you never reconcile by hand.

04What is the client portal on Team?

Each client gets a private link where they can see every invoice and quote you’ve sent them, download PDFs and pay outstanding balances. No more “could you resend that invoice from March?”

05Can I change plans or cancel later?

Yes, from settings, at any time. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. If you cancel or downgrade, you keep your paid features until the end of the billing month, and you can export all your invoices and data whenever you like.

06Do prices include VAT?

Prices are shown excluding VAT. Tax is added at checkout based on your country, and if you have a valid EU VAT number, reverse charge applies. You’ll receive a proper invoice from us every month, naturally.
