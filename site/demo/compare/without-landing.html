FAQ

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@tally.example — a real person replies, usually within a few hours on weekdays.

Will my clients know reminders are automated?

Reminders are sent from your own email address and signed with your name, using wording you’ve approved. Replies land in your inbox, not ours. Most clients simply see a polite, well-timed note from you.

Is connecting my bank account safe?

Tally connects through licensed PSD2 open banking providers with read-only access. We can see incoming transactions so we can match them to invoices — we can’t initiate payments or move money. You can revoke access from your bank or from Tally at any time.

Are Tally invoices legally valid in my country?

Yes. Tally includes the mandatory mentions for each EU member state, the UK, Norway and Switzerland, keeps numbering sequential and gap-free, and archives every invoice for the legally required retention period (up to 10 years).

What happens when a client pays only part of an invoice?

Tally records the partial payment, updates the balance due, and adjusts the next reminder so it asks for the remaining amount — not the full total.

Can I import my existing clients and invoices?

Import clients from CSV, and past invoices from most popular tools. Your existing numbering series carries over, so your next invoice continues exactly where you left off.

Do you take a cut of my payments?

No. Clients pay you directly by bank transfer, and the money goes straight to your account. If you turn on optional card payments, only the payment provider’s standard fee applies — Tally adds nothing on top.
