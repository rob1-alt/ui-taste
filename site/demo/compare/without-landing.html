Questions

Things freelancers ask first

Will reminders upset my clients?

Reminders go out under your name, in your client’s language, and you choose the tone and timing. The default sequence is deliberately gentle. You can preview every message and pause any client in one click.

Can Tally access or move money in my bank account?

No. The bank connection is read-only and uses open banking (PSD2), so Tally can see incoming transactions to match them but can’t initiate payments. If your bank isn’t supported, you can upload a statement file instead.

What if a payment can’t be matched?

Tally never guesses on uncertain cases. Partial payments, overpayments and missing references land in a short review list, with a suggested invoice you can confirm in one tap.

Does Tally handle VAT and my local requirements?

Tally supports domestic VAT, EU reverse charge and small-business exemptions, and prints the required notices on the invoice. Rules vary by country and situation, so check with your accountant for anything unusual. They can have free access to your account.

Can I bring my existing clients and invoices?

Yes. Import clients and past invoices from a CSV file, so reminders and bank matching work on open invoices from day one.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose a plan or stop. We don’t ask for a card up front, and you can export all your invoices and client data at any time, whether you stay or not.

Set up in about ten minutes. Your next invoice can go out today, and the reminder after it will take care of itself.
