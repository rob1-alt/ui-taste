Questions, answered

The short version of what people ask before they pick a plan.

What counts as one of the 5 invoices on Solo?

Each invoice you send to a client in a calendar month. Drafts, quotes and invoices you delete before sending don't count. The count resets on the first of every month.

What happens when I reach the limit?

Nothing breaks. Your sent invoices, clients and history stay where they are. You can keep writing drafts, and send them when the month resets or as soon as you move to Studio.

How do automatic reminders work?

You choose when reminders go out, for example three days before the due date and then a week after it. Tally emails the client from your account and stops as soon as the invoice is paid.

What is bank matching?

Connect your business bank account and Tally reads incoming payments, matches each one to the open invoice it settles, and marks it paid. You confirm anything it isn't sure about.

What does the client portal do?

Each client gets a private link listing every invoice you've sent them, what's paid and what's outstanding, with PDFs to download. No more "can you resend that invoice from March?"

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, at any time from your account settings. Paid plans are monthly with no commitment. If you go back to Solo, your data stays and the 5-invoice limit applies again from the next month.

Are prices shown with VAT?

No. Prices are in euros and exclude VAT, which is added at checkout based on your country. Businesses with a valid EU VAT number are billed under reverse charge.
