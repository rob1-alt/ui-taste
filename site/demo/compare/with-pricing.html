Questions

Something else? Write to us from the help page and a person will answer.

Is Solo really free, or is it a trial?

It is free with no end date. You can send 5 invoices every month for as long as you like, and no card is needed to sign up.

What happens when I need a sixth invoice in a month?

Tally asks you to upgrade before sending it. Your drafts, clients and past invoices stay exactly where they are. If you'd rather wait, your count resets on the 1st of the next month.

How do automatic reminders work?

When an invoice passes its due date and hasn't been paid, Tally emails your client a short reminder with the invoice attached. You choose when reminders go out and can turn them off for any client.

What does bank matching do?

Connect your business account and Tally reads incoming payments. When one matches an open invoice by amount and reference, the invoice is marked paid and the reminders stop. Anything uncertain waits for you to confirm.

Is Team €29 per person?

No. €29 a month covers the whole workspace with up to 5 people in it. Everyone shares the same clients, invoices and bank matching.

Can I change plans or cancel later?

Yes, from your workspace settings at any time. If you move back to Solo, every invoice you've sent stays in your account; only the paid features switch off.

Do prices include VAT?

Prices are shown before VAT. VAT is added at checkout depending on your country and whether you have a VAT number, and it appears on the invoice Tally sends you.
