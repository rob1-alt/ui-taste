Questions, answered

Something missing? Write to us and a person will reply.

What happens when I reach 5 invoices on Solo?

You keep full access to everything you've already created. To send a sixth invoice in the same month, upgrade to Studio, or wait for your count to reset at the start of the next month.

What does bank matching do?

Tally compares incoming payments on your bank account with your open invoices and marks the matching ones as paid, so you don't have to tick them off by hand.

How do automatic reminders work?

When an invoice passes its due date, Tally emails your client a polite nudge for you. Once the invoice is paid, the reminders stop.

What counts as a seat on the Team plan?

A seat is one person who signs in to your Tally account to create or manage invoices. Team includes five. Your clients never use a seat, whether or not they use the client portal.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Move between Solo, Studio and Team whenever you like from your account settings, and cancel at any time. If you drop back to Solo, you keep your invoice history.
