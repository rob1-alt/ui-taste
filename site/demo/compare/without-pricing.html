Questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email us and a real person will reply.

What happens when I reach 5 invoices on Solo?

You can keep editing drafts and tracking the invoices you’ve already sent. New invoices unlock again on the first of next month, or straight away if you upgrade to Studio.

How does bank matching work?

Connect your business bank account and Tally compares incoming payments with your open invoices. When a payment lines up, the invoice is marked as paid and any scheduled reminders stop.

Will reminders annoy my clients?

You choose when they go out and what they say. Reminders only go to clients with an overdue invoice, and they stop as soon as the invoice is paid.

Can I change plans or cancel later?

Yes, any time from your settings. Upgrades take effect immediately. If you downgrade or cancel, your paid plan stays active until the end of the current billing month, and your invoices remain yours to export.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices are shown excluding VAT, which is added at checkout based on your country. If you have a valid EU VAT number, you can add it to your account.

We’re more than 5 people. Can we add seats?

Team includes 5 seats. If your studio needs more, get in touch and we’ll set you up.
