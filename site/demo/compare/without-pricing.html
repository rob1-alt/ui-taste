What happens when I reach 5 invoices on Solo?

You can keep using Tally, but you won't be able to create more invoices until the next month, or until you upgrade to Studio. Your existing invoices and clients stay untouched.

What does bank matching do?

Tally compares incoming payments with your open invoices and marks them as paid, so you don't have to reconcile by hand. Automatic reminders stop as soon as an invoice is matched.

How do automatic reminders work?

When an invoice goes overdue, Tally emails your client a polite reminder for you. You stay in control of when they go out and what they say.

Who counts as a seat on Team?

Each person who signs in to your Tally workspace uses one seat. Team includes five. Your clients don't take a seat: they use the client portal for free.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Switch between plans or cancel from your account settings at any time. There's no contract, and you can always go back to Solo.
