Questions

Will my clients know the reminders are automatic?

They come from your email address, signed by you, in the words you wrote. Replies land in your own inbox. Tally doesn’t add its own branding to your emails.

Can I keep a client out of reminders?

Yes. Pause reminders for one client, one invoice, or everything while you’re on holiday. You can also ask Tally to show you each reminder before it goes out.

Can Tally move money out of my account?

No. The bank connection is read-only. Tally sees incoming transactions so it can match them to invoices. It cannot make payments or transfers.

What happens when a payment can’t be matched?

It waits in a short list with Tally’s best guess next to it. One click confirms or reassigns it. Nothing is marked paid until the match is certain or you’ve confirmed it.

Do I still need an accountant?

Probably, for your tax return. Tally handles invoicing and getting paid. Your accountant can export invoices and payments as CSV or PDF at any time.
