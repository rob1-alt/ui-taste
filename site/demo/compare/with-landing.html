Questions, answered straight.

Who is Tally for?

Freelance designers, developers and small studios in Europe who invoice a handful to a few dozen clients and would rather not spend Friday afternoons chasing them.

Will Tally email my clients without asking me?

Only on the schedule you set, and with wording you’ve approved. You choose which reminders are on, and you can switch them off for any client or invoice.

How does it know a payment is for a given invoice?

It compares each incoming bank payment with your open invoices, using the amount and the payment reference. When it’s confident, it matches. When it isn’t, it asks you.

What happens when a client pays late, after a reminder?

The payment is matched, the invoice is marked paid and any reminders still queued are cancelled. Nobody gets chased for money they’ve already sent.

When can I use it, and what will it cost?

Tally is opening to a small group first. Leave your email and you’ll hear from us when your turn comes. Pricing will be shared before anyone is asked to pay.
