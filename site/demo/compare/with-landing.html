Questions

Which banks can Tally read payments from?

Tally connects through regulated open-banking access, which most business and personal accounts in the EU and UK support. The connection is read-only: Tally can see incoming payments, it can never move money.

Will reminders sound like a robot wrote them?

They go out from your address, signed with your name, in the tone you pick. You can rewrite any of them, per client or for everyone, and see each one before it is sent if you prefer.

Can I stop reminders for a client I'd rather call?

Yes. Pause reminders for one invoice or one client, or turn them off entirely. Paid invoices never get a reminder.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They receive an email with the PDF and a payment page. They can pay by bank transfer with the reference pre-filled, or by card.

Can I charge late-payment fees?

For business clients in the EU, the Late Payment Directive allows a fixed €40 compensation plus statutory interest once an invoice is overdue. Tally can add this to the final notice. It is off by default.

Can I bring my existing invoices?

Import your clients and open invoices from a CSV file. Tally continues your numbering where you left off.
