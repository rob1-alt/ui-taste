Straight answers, with proof.

Will my auditor accept a report produced by Fleuret?

The report is audit evidence, not a certification. Your auditor decides what it proves. It lists what was tested and held as well as what was found, with a replayable proof behind every finding.

How is this different from a vulnerability scanner?

A scanner matches signatures and reports what might be vulnerable. Fleuret executes each step of an attack and reports what it actually exploited. Every finding comes with a proof you can replay yourself, which is why a Fleuret report carries fewer false positives than a scanner export, and is usually shorter.

What can you test today, and what can't you?

Today: web applications, REST and GraphQL APIs, and external infrastructure. Not yet: Active Directory, mobile applications and cloud pentests are on the roadmap. If your audit needs them this quarter, say so on the first call and you will get a straight answer.

What do you need from us to start?

Three things: the scope (URLs, and an API specification if you have one), test accounts for each role you want tested, and a signed authorisation that defines what is in and out of scope and the testing window. We never test a system without written authorisation from its owner.

Do you re-test after we fix?

Yes, and a re-test of a finding costs no credit. On the one-off plan re-tests are unlimited for 90 days; on the yearly plans they are unlimited. The report is updated with the new status of each finding.
